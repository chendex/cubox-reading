Why Deep Reading Is Getting Rarer

为什么深度阅读越来越罕见

Most of what we save never gets read. The folder grows, the guilt grows with it, and the articles we meant to treasure become a backlog we avoid.

我们保存的大部分内容从未被阅读。文件夹不断变大，负罪感也随之增长，那些我们本想珍视的文章变成了一堆我们避之不及的积压。

Deep reading is not about speed. It is about staying with a difficult paragraph long enough for the argument to actually land, instead of skimming for keywords.

深度阅读不在于速度。它在于在一个难懂的段落上停留足够长的时间，让论证真正落地，而不是快速浏览关键词。

  • Skimming gives you the shape of an argument.
    略读让你得到论证的轮廓。
  • Reading gives you the argument itself.
    阅读让你得到论证本身。
The bottleneck is almost never access to information. It is attention.

瓶颈几乎从来不是获取信息。而是注意力。

That is why annotation tools matter: they turn a passive scroll into a conversation with the text, and a conversation is what you remember.

这就是批注工具重要的原因：它们把被动的滚动变成与文本的对话，而对话才是你能记住的东西。
